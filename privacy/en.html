Lumo Privacy Policy

EnglishРусскийEspañolFrançais

Effective: November 3, 2026

Summary

Lumo is a photo and video editor that works on your device. We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell your personal data. Lumo has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Photos and videos

Lumo asks for access to your photo library so you can choose and edit your photos and videos. You can allow full or limited access.

Editing, masks, effects and exports run on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. An edited photo or video is added to your library only when you choose to save or export it.

To let you continue later, Lumo keeps edit settings (history, presets and recipes) on your device.

Microphone and speech recognition

These are used only if you use voice input in Smart Assist. Speech is recognized on your device with Apple's on-device speech recognition. If your language is not supported on device, voice input is not offered. Audio is not recorded or sent anywhere. You can always type your request instead.

Smart Assist

Text commands are interpreted on your device, using built-in rules and, where available, Apple's on-device language model. Your requests are not sent to us.

Purchases

Lumo Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your payment details, name or Apple ID; the app only checks on your device whether your subscription is active.

Data stored on your device

Language and theme settings, edit sessions, presets and recipes stay on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

Third parties

Lumo does not use third-party SDKs or services. Apple provides the App Store and iOS services under its own privacy policy.

Children

Lumo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your choices

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can change or revoke access to Photos, Microphone and Speech Recognition at any time in iOS Settings > Lumo.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/Alberto22m/lumo-privacy/issues. Please do not post personal information in a public issue.
